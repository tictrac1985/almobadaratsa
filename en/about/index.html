About Us

We don't just deliver temporary initiatives — we build impact that lasts.

Almobadarat (Development Initiatives Company) was founded in 2015 to contribute to advancing development work in the Kingdom through solutions that combine research, strategy, and execution.

We work with companies, institutions, and various entities to turn development and social-responsibility goals into executable, measurable programs and initiatives — from needs assessment and initiative design, through planning and execution, to measuring impact and sustainability.

The company also provides economic and development studies, and works to develop national initiatives and high-quality projects that address social and economic needs and turn them into sustainable institutional solutions.

Our business model has evolved to bring together economic, managerial, and development expertise, allowing us to treat every initiative as an integrated project that begins with an idea and ends with measuring its impact and sustainability.

A Message From Our CEO

Chief Executive Officer

Almobadarat – Development Initiatives Company

At Almobadarat, we believe real development is not measured by the amount of money spent, but by our ability to turn those resources into lasting impact.

Since the company's founding in 2015, we have worked to develop a business model that combines professional planning and management with a deep understanding of community needs and the building of partnerships across sectors.

Through our work, we aim to develop initiatives that go beyond temporary solutions and contribute to building models, institutions, and programs capable of enduring, growing, and creating real value for the community.

We always look forward to working with our partners in the government, private, and nonprofit sectors to develop new initiatives that keep pace with the Kingdom's ambitions and help build a more sustainable future.
